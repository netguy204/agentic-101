# Practice: one week with the sheet

<!-- Chunk: docs/chunks/fluency_session_01 - Session 1 practice assignment -->

**About 55 minutes, spread across a working week.**

The rows you wrote from memory miss the small things. A week of noticing
what you actually do finds them.

**Aim for:** 8–10 rows by the end of the week, each with columns 3 and 4
filled. You need at least three to submit; more gives you real choices in
Session 6.

| When | How long | What |
|---|---|---|
| End of each working day (×4) | 5 min | The daily pass |
| Once, mid-week | 15 min | The check test |
| Once, at the end of the week | 20 min | The consolidation pass, then submit |

---

## 1. The daily pass (5 minutes × 4)

At the end of each working day, ask:

> **What did I do today that I've done before?**

Add each answer as a row, columns 1 and 2 only. Leave columns 3 and 4 for
the consolidation pass.

Two kinds of task people usually miss:

- **Waiting and chasing:** following up, nudging, checking whether someone
  did the thing.
- **The work before the work:** getting a file into shape, fixing someone
  else's formatting, finding last month's version.

If a day produces nothing, write "nothing new".

---

## 2. The check test (15 minutes, once)

1. Pick **two rows marked *not sure***.
2. The next time you do one of those tasks, notice what you actually look
   at to know it is right: a single total, a name that should be on the
   list, a date that is often wrong, a row that once caught you out.
3. Write that in column 4, and change column 3 to *in* or *out*.

If you have no *not sure* rows, pick two *in* rows and test whether their
checks are what you really look at.

---

## 3. The consolidation pass (20 minutes, before Session 2)

Go over the whole sheet once:

1. **Fill in columns 3 and 4 for every row added this week**, using the
   definitions in [`handouts/toil-inventory.md`](handouts/toil-inventory.md).
2. **Split any row too big to picture** as one activity.
3. **Add the time:** roughly how long and how often, for every row. Session
   6 uses this; a task that takes 20 minutes twice a year is a poor final
   project.
4. **Star your three most annoying rows.** These are the ones you will most
   want automated after the course ends.
5. **Submit** your sheet and your one-task explanation (see the Session 1
   index for what to hand in).

---

## Optional: a page on one task

Pick one *in* row and write half a page for a new colleague on how to tell
whether this work was done right: what good looks like, the common
mistakes, and what you would never sign off. In Session 5 you will use
much the same page to tell an agent what you expect.

---

## Before Session 2

Session 2 needs the software you installed. If it isn't working, contact
your instructor ("Stuck on this session?", then "Send to your instructor")
before you start Session 2.

## If the week gets away from you

Do the consolidation pass and submit. Three rows with columns 3 and 4
filled are enough.
