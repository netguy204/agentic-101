# Practice: a week with your toil inventory sheet

<!-- Chunk: docs/chunks/fluency_session_01 - Session 1 practice assignment -->

**About 55 minutes, spread across a working week.**

The rows you wrote from memory will have missed the small things. A week of
noticing what you actually do will find them. Keep your
[toil inventory sheet](handouts/toil-inventory.md#the-sheet) somewhere you'll
see it during the week.

**Aim for** 8 to 10 rows by the end of the week, each with the third and
fourth columns filled in. You need at least three to submit, and more will
give you real choices in Session 6.

| When | How long | What |
|---|---|---|
| End of each working day (four days) | 5 min | The daily pass |
| Once, mid-week | 15 min | The check test |
| Once, at the end of the week | 20 min | The consolidation pass, then submit |

---

## 1. The daily pass (5 minutes × 4)

**You'll need:** your toil inventory sheet.

At the end of each working day, ask yourself:

> **What did I do today that I've done before?**

Add each answer as a new row. Fill in only the first two columns, *The task,
and how often* and *What "done right" looks like*. You'll come back to the
other two in the consolidation pass.

Two kinds of task are easy to miss:

- **Waiting and chasing.** Following up, nudging, checking whether someone
  did the thing.
- **The work before the work.** Getting a file into shape, fixing someone
  else's formatting, finding last month's version.

If a day turns up nothing, write "nothing new" and move on.

---

## 2. The check test (15 minutes, once)

**You'll need:** your toil inventory sheet.

1. Pick **two rows you marked *not sure***.
2. The next time you do one of those tasks, notice what you actually look at
   to know it's right. It might be a single total, a name that should be on
   the list, a date that's often wrong, or a row that once caught you out.
3. Write that in the fourth column, *How I'd check*, and change the third
   column to *in* or *out*.

If you don't have any *not sure* rows, pick two *in* rows instead and test
whether their checks are what you really look at.

---

## 3. The consolidation pass (20 minutes, before Session 2)

**You'll need:** your toil inventory sheet, and the
[column definitions](handouts/toil-inventory.md#the-four-columns).

Go over the whole sheet once:

1. **Fill in the third and fourth columns** for every row you added this
   week.
2. **Split any row that's too big** to picture as one activity.
3. **Add the time** for every row: roughly how long it takes and how often.
   Session 6 uses this, because a task that takes 20 minutes twice a year
   makes a poor final project.
4. **Star your three most annoying rows.** These are the ones you'll most
   want automated by the end of the course.
5. **Submit** your sheet and your one-task explanation. The
   [Session 1 overview](../../platform/index/01_mental_models.md#what-youll-submit)
   lists exactly what's needed.

---

## Optional: a page on one task

Pick one *in* row and write half a page for a new colleague on how to tell
whether this work was done right: what good looks like, the usual mistakes,
and what you'd never sign off. In Session 5 you'll use much the same page to
tell an agent what you expect.

## If the week gets away from you

Do the consolidation pass and submit. Three rows with the third and fourth
columns filled in are enough.
