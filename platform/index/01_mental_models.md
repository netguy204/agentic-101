# Session 1 — Mental models and the toil inventory

<!-- Chunk: docs/chunks/platform_student_index - The student's index for Session 1, written from its README and (then) PREWORK -->

Welcome to the course. This first session is about deciding what's worth
handing to an agent. You'll make a list of the work in your job that
repeats, and next to each task you'll write down how you'd check an agent's
result. You won't need the terminal today. The software you installed comes
in at Session 2.

## What you'll submit

1. **Your [toil inventory sheet](../../curriculum/session_01_mental_models/handouts/toil-inventory.md#the-sheet).** It needs at least three rows with all
   four columns filled in, each marked *in*, *out* or *not sure*. Every row
   you mark *in* should name a specific check, such as a total you'd add up
   again, a name you'd recognise, or a person who'd complain. "I'd read it
   over" doesn't count as a check yet. For a row you mark *out*, say why you
   couldn't check it.
2. **One task, explained.** Pick one row and, in a few sentences, say
   whether it's inside or outside what you can judge, and how you'd know if
   the result were wrong. You can type it or record a voice note.

You'll need both to complete the session.

## Steps

| Step | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. [Prework](../prework/01_mental_models.md) | 10 min | Write down five things you did more than once last week. |
| 2. The audio | 15 min | Four short parts. Listen, or read the same material if you prefer. |
| 3. Fill in your toil inventory sheet | 25 min | Copy your five tasks onto the [sheet](../../curriculum/session_01_mental_models/handouts/toil-inventory.md#the-sheet) and add more. Then mark each one *in*, *out* or *not sure*, and write how you'd check it. |
| 4. Skeptic pass | 12 min | Take two or three of your *in* rows and ask "how would I know if it was wrong?" Expect to change at least one. |
| 5. Daily pass | 5 min, four days | At the end of each day, add anything you did again. |
| 6. Check test | 15 min | Settle two *not sure* rows by checking them for real. |
| 7. Consolidate and submit | 20 min | Read through the whole sheet once, star your three most annoying tasks, and submit. |

Steps 5 to 7 are spread over about a week. You can stop after any step and
pick up at the next one.

## Getting help

Some of the help in this course is automated, and a person is always there
too. Here's what's available, roughly in the order you'd reach for it:

1. **The material.** Each handout explains its columns and terms, and most
   steps have a "Stuck?" line.
2. **The course assistant.** It's automated, and it knows every exercise in
   the course. You can open it from "Stuck on this session?" on any session
   page.
3. **The checkpoint conversation.** If your answer is close, you'll get a
   follow-up question and a second try.
4. **Your instructor.** This is a person, and they see anything the
   assistant can't sort out. To reach them directly, choose "Stuck on this
   session?" on any session page, then "Send to your instructor". They'll
   reply in the same thread, usually within a day, and if a call would help
   they'll offer one.

If the course software won't work, or you're not sure whether you may use a
real task from your job, go straight to your instructor.
