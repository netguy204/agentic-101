# Session 1 — Mental models and the toil inventory

<!-- Chunk: docs/chunks/platform_student_index - The student's index for Session 1, written from its README and PREWORK -->

In this session you decide what is worth handing to an agent. You list the
repeating work in your job, and next to each task you write down how you
would check an agent's result. There is no terminal in this session. The
software you installed comes in at Session 2.

## What you hand in

1. **Your toil inventory sheet.** At least three rows with all four
   columns filled, each marked *in*, *out* or *not sure*. Every *in* row
   names a specific check: a total you would re-add, a name you would
   recognise, a person who would complain. "I'd read it over" is not a
   check. An *out* row says why you couldn't check it.
2. **One task, explained.** Pick one row. In a few sentences, typed or as a
   voice note, say whether it is inside or outside what you can judge, and
   how you would know if the result were wrong.

You need both to pass.

## Steps

| Step | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Prework | 10 min | Write down five things you did more than once last week. |
| 2. Concept track | 15 min | Four short segments. Listen or read; no screen needed for listening. |
| 3. Fill the sheet | 25 min | Copy your five onto the sheet, add more, then mark each *in*, *out* or *not sure* and write its check. |
| 4. Skeptic pass | 12 min | Ask "how would I know if it was wrong?" of two or three *in* rows. Expect to change at least one. |
| 5. Daily pass | 5 min, four days | At the end of each day, add anything you did again. |
| 6. Check test | 15 min | Resolve two *not sure* rows by actually checking. |
| 7. Consolidate and submit | 20 min | Go over the whole sheet once, star your three most annoying tasks, and hand in. |

Steps 5 to 7 spread across about a week. You can stop after any step and
pick up at the next one.

## Getting help

Some help in this course is automated, and a person is always available.
Use whichever you need, in roughly this order:

1. **The material.** Each handout explains its columns and terms, and most
   steps have a "Stuck?" line.
2. **The course assistant.** It is automated, and it knows every exercise
   in the course.
3. **The checkpoint conversation.** If your answer is close, you get a
   follow-up question and a second try.
4. **Your facilitator,** a person. They see anything the assistant can't
   resolve, and you can contact them directly at any time.

Go straight to your facilitator if the course software won't work, or if
you are unsure whether you may use a real task from your job.
