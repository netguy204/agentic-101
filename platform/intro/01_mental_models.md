# Session 1 — Mental models and the toil inventory

<!-- Chunk: docs/chunks/platform_student_index - The session intro shown under the title on the session page -->

Welcome to the course. This first session is about deciding what's worth
handing to an agent. You'll make a list of the work in your job that
repeats, and next to each task you'll write down how you'd check an agent's
result. You won't need the terminal today. The software you installed comes
in at Session 2.
