# Before Session 1

<!-- Chunk: docs/chunks/platform_cohort_prework - The self-paced student's prework; Session 1's only prework (DEC-014) -->

**Time:** about 10 minutes.

Think back over your last working week, the actual week rather than a
typical one, and write down **five things you did more than once**, or do
every week. The tedious end of your job is the place to look.

If you get stuck, these questions usually turn something up:

- What did you do on Monday morning before any real work?
- What do you copy from one place into another?
- What do you chase people for?
- What do you reformat?
- What do you check every time, in case someone got it wrong?
- What would you hand a new colleague first because it's tedious and
  low-risk?

Rough notes are fine, because you'll rewrite them in the lab. Keep the list
somewhere you'll have it when you get to step 3, filling in your
[toil inventory sheet](../../curriculum/session_01_mental_models/handouts/toil-inventory.md#the-sheet).

**If your work is confidential,** use
[Priya's sample tasks](../../curriculum/session_01_mental_models/handouts/canned-toil.md#priyas-recurring-tasks)
for the whole session instead. You'll learn just as much.
