# Before Session 1

<!-- Chunk: docs/chunks/platform_cohort_prework - The self-paced student's prework; Session 1's only prework (DEC-014) -->

**Time:** 10 minutes.

Think back over your last working week: the actual week, not a typical
one. Write down **five things you did more than once**, or do every week.
Pick the tedious end of your job.

If you get stuck, these usually turn something up:

- What did you do on Monday morning before any real work?
- What do you copy from one place into another?
- What do you chase people for?
- What do you reformat?
- What do you check every time, in case someone got it wrong?
- What would you hand a new colleague first because it is tedious and
  low-risk?

Rough notes are fine; you will rewrite them in the lab. Keep the list
somewhere you will have it when you start Step 3.

**If your work is confidential**, use the prepared set of tasks in
[`canned-toil.md`](../../curriculum/session_01_mental_models/handouts/canned-toil.md)
for the whole session. It produces the same result.
